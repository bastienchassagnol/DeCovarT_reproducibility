This book applies DeCovarT to the Suppinger murine gastruloid resource. It records what is run: the data, the algorithms as implemented, the hyperparameters and their values, the code, the outputs, and which outputs are stale. The reasons behind each choice are in the DeCovarT vignettes; each chapter links to the vignette section that states them and does not restate the theory.

0.1 Aims

  1. Estimate, for each labelled cell type j at each bulk-matched time, the mean \widehat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_{\cdot j} and the within-type covariance \widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}_{W,j} of a gene panel, on the linear scale that DeCovarT’s convolution needs. The source is one single-specimen scRNA-seq reference (GSE229513, Section 2.1).
  2. Choose that panel for the purpose: genes whose moments can be estimated in every type and that carry information about the proportions \boldsymbol{p} (Section 3.1).
  3. Plug the moments into DeCovarT and deconvolve the bulk series (GSE229386) where a single-cell reference matches it: the standard-Wnt samples at 48 h, 72 h and 96 h.

The target is a covariance plug-in for the likelihood, not a biologically interpretable gene regulatory network (03-2, why a regularised covariance). Indices follow the article: gene g=1,\ldots,G, type j=1,\ldots,J, bulk sample i=1,\ldots,N, cell c, with C_j cells of type j.

0.2 Pipeline

Figure 1 lists the nine steps in the order the scripts run them. Steps 2 to 5 are Section 3.1; step 7 is Section 4.1; step 8 is 2.1, three regimes; step 9 is Section 5.2.

## Aims {#sec-aims}

1. Estimate, for each labelled cell type $j$ at each bulk-matched time,
   the mean $\widehat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_{\cdot j}$ and the within-type
   covariance $\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}_{W,j}$ of a gene panel, on
   the linear scale that DeCovarT's convolution needs. The source is
   one single-specimen scRNA-seq reference (GSE229513,
   @sec-geo-design).
2. Choose that panel for the purpose: genes whose moments can be
   estimated in every type and that carry information about the
   proportions $\boldsymbol{p}$ (@sec-fs-scope).
3. Plug the moments into DeCovarT and deconvolve the bulk series
   (GSE229386) where a single-cell reference matches it: the
   standard-Wnt samples at 48 h, 72 h and 96 h.

The target is a covariance plug-in for the likelihood, not a
biologically interpretable gene regulatory network
([03-2, why a regularised covariance](https://bastienchassagnol.github.io/DeCovarT/articles/03-2-covariance-network-estimation.html#nte-cov-why)).
Indices follow the article: gene $g=1,\ldots,G$, type
$j=1,\ldots,J$, bulk sample $i=1,\ldots,N$, cell $c$, with $C_j$ cells
of type $j$.

## Pipeline {#sec-pipeline}

@fig-pipeline lists the nine steps in the order the scripts run them.
Steps 2 to 5 are @sec-fs-scope; step 7 is @sec-cov-scope; step 8 is
[2.1, three regimes](https://bastienchassagnol.github.io/DeCovarT/articles/02-1-single-cell-generative-optimisation.html#sec-three-regimes);
step 9 is @sec-deconv-models.

Row 2 estimates one covariance per type and time on the linear scale, with the tuning parameter of each estimator chosen by cross-validated held-out Gaussian log-likelihood, lifts the estimates to DeCovarT's moments and deconvolves the matched bulk at all three times. Each chapter is the practice half of a vignette chapter. Read the
vignette for the model and the reasons; read the chapter for what is
run on Suppinger.

| Book chapter | What it runs | Theory (DeCovarT vignettes) |
|---|---|---|
| @sec-geo-design, Suppinger data | GEO objects, design, Seurat processing | [1.1 Generative model](https://bastienchassagnol.github.io/DeCovarT/articles/01-1-population-generative-optimisation.html); [2.1 Three reference regimes](https://bastienchassagnol.github.io/DeCovarT/articles/02-1-single-cell-generative-optimisation.html#sec-three-regimes) |
| @sec-fs-scope, Feature selection | tier (i) QC, markers and presence gate; tier (ii) four tests of the type effect and deviance rank; tier (iii) outliers and bulk tail filter | [3.1 Feature selection](https://bastienchassagnol.github.io/DeCovarT/articles/03-1-feature-selection.html#wrn-fs-estimand) |
