     1. Figure fig-kappa-gastruloid (TikZ, portable PDF)

     - Source: docs/images/src/kappa_gastruloid/kappa_gastruloid.tex. It is
       standalone, LuaLaTeX only, with a header comment giving the build
       commands, as in docs/images/src/early_wnt/early_wnt.tex.
       - Preamble: the font block of the graphical-models and latex-scientific
         rules (STIX Two Math, TeX Gyre Heros with Ligatures=CommonOff, \symbf,
         no bm). Then bayesnet,positioning,fit,calc,arrows.meta, plus the fixed,
         note and dependency styles and the ink and obs colours of
         docs/_quarto.yml.
       - Arrows use \draw[dependency], never \edge. Equations go only in note
         nodes.
     - Row A, notation:
       - Nodes: obs x_gc, const z_c, det μ_gj (the signature S), obs y_gi and
         latent p_i.
       - Plates: c, g and i.
       - Note nodes: $\symbf y_i\approx\symbf S\symbf p_i$, $p_{ji}\ge0$,
         $\sum_jp_{ji}=1$.
       - A lineage strip of boxes: epiblast → primitive streak → caudal
         mesoderm/NMP → PSM → somite.
     - Row B, reference building per method:
       - SCDC: μ_gj det, with one reference (R = 1, no ensemble).
       - BayesPrism: latent φ_j with a Dirichlet prior, and φ_gj as the read
         share.
       - DWLS: μ_gj → det marker set M, chosen by minimising κ(S) = σ_max/σ_min
         over g ∈ {50,…,200}, with MAST and diff.cutoff = 0.5.
     - Row C, inference:
       - SCDC: $\min_{p\ge0,\sum p=1}\sum_gw_g(y_{gi}-\symbf S_g\symbf p)^2$
         with $w_g=1/(\nu+\hat\delta_g^2)$.
       - BayesPrism: latent read assignments in a plate over reads, Gibbs
         sampling, posterior $P(\symbf p_i\mid\symbf y_i)$.
       - DWLS: dampened weights $1/\hat y_{gi}^2$ on g ∈ M.
       - One hypothesis box: "BayesPrism ≈ SCDC ≠ DWLS: κ-driven pruning drops
         the genes that separate collinear lineages".
     - Formulas: they follow @tbl-deconv-own-genes, not the simplified AI
       picture.
     - Build:
       a. lualatex produces the PDF.
       b. gs -dNoOutputFonts turns every glyph into an outline. The result is
          the portable PDF docs/images/fig_04_kappa_gastruloid.pdf.
       c. pdftocairo -svg makes fig_04_kappa_gastruloid.svg, which the HTML book
          embeds because it cannot show a PDF inline. Then delete the white
          page rectangle in the SVG.
       d. No editable SVG and no TexText. This follows the user's "portable PDF
          only".
