Single-cell UMI counts and bulk HTSeq tables come from the murine gastruloid resource of Suppinger and colleagues (Cell Stem Cell, 2023; GEO GSE229513 and GSE229386; (Suppinger et al. 2023)).

The scRNA-seq experiment is one 10x Chromium 3′ v3 resource from one specimen series, not a panel of independent biological replicates. DeCovarT treats it as the one-replicate regime (2.1, one single-cell replicate). Zeros are taken as molecule-sampling counts (?nte-drop-zico). GSE250136 is a later stem-embryo dataset and is not part of this resource.

2.1 Hierarchical design

Figure 2.1 draws the two designs side by side.

Single-cell (GSE229513). The first factor is the mESC cell line (B-S or SBR), the second the time slot. Each line covers six slots, and only 48 h is profiled in both. At the three times of the bulk series the cell counts are (output/naive_marker_selection/tables/batch_by_time.csv):

Cell line is therefore confounded with time except at 48 h, and it is not a covariate in any fit of this book. The two lines are treated as one developmental trajectory (Figure 2.4). Gene ranking and covariance estimation run independently at 48 h, 72 h and 96 h, the slots with a bulk time point (Section 3.3.1, Section 4.1). At 48 h the two lines are pooled; Section 3.7.1 measures what pooling changes.

Bulk RNA-seq (GSE229386). The first factor is the Wnt schedule: the standard pulse at 48 h, or an early pulse at 24 h. The second factor is the time point, 48 h, 72 h or 96 h, each with three biological replicates. No single-cell slot carries the early schedule, so the early-Wnt bulk has no single-cell counterpart at any time. The three single-cell slots used in this book instead match the three time points of the standard-Wnt bulk: single-cell 48 h (B-S and SBR pooled), 72 h (SBR) and 96 h (B-S), each against three biological replicates of the standard-Wnt bulk at that time. The cross-platform filter of Section 3.4 is therefore fitted at all three standard-Wnt time points, never on the early-Wnt arm.

2.2 Seurat processing

Embeddings, QC and clustering follow the Seurat workflow of the Suppinger resource (Suppinger et al. 2023), drawn in Figure 2.2: Alevin counts, scater QC, SCTransform Pearson residuals per batch for integration, CCA, PCA, UMAP and Louvain clustering. The deposited object (Seurat 4.1.0) holds the raw counts (RNA, 27,027 genes, 71,005 cells), the SCT layer and the integrated assay. The SCT and integrated layers keep the same 71,005 cells and only 2,944 genes: the variable features of the CCA integration, chosen over all twelve time points and both batches. Two products of that workflow are used here: the cell-type labels (celltypeannotation), and that 2,944-gene list, which was the gene allow-list of the legacy panel only; the current tier (i) starts from the raw counts and records the list for comparison (Section 3.2). The authors’ residuals themselves are not used: the ranking fits its own negative-binomial GLMs per time slot (Section 3.3.1), the legacy panel refitted SCTransform v2 per slot (legacy SCTransform fit), and the covariances are estimated from raw counts (Section 4.1.1).

2.3 Biological interpretation from single-cell compositions

Figure 2.3 and Figure 2.4 summarise a putative reading of annotated type proportions along gastruloid time. They are not a reconstruction of embryonic lineages. Two annotated states are treated as technical artefacts of culture stress (Cd63+ ectoderm-like artefact and Zscan4+ artefact, data/dictionaries/suppinger_celltype_crosswalk.csv). The working caudal sequence after the Chir/Wnt pulse is pluripotency \to epiblast \to primitive streak \to caudal mesoderm / NMP \to pre-somitic mesoderm / somites, with a residual endoderm bias typical of gastruloids.

The early-versus-standard Wnt reading of bulk compositions is in Section 6.1, after the mean-based comparators of Section 5.1.
