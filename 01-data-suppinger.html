2  Suppinger gastruloids

Single-cell UMI counts and bulk HTSeq tables come from the murine gastruloid resource of Suppinger and colleagues (Cell Stem Cell, 2023; GEO GSE229513 and GSE229386; (Suppinger et al. 2023)).

The scRNA-seq experiment is one 10x Chromium 3′ v3 resource from one specimen series, not a panel of independent biological replicates. DeCovarT treats it as the one-replicate regime (2.1, one single-cell replicate). Zeros are taken as molecule-sampling counts (Note 4.1). GSE250136 is a later stem-embryo dataset and is not part of this resource.

2.1 Hierarchical design

Figure 2.1 draws the two designs side by side.

Single-cell (GSE229513). The first factor is the mESC cell line (B-S or SBR), the second the time slot. Each line covers six slots, and only 48 h is profiled in both. At the three times of the bulk series the cell counts are (output/naive_marker_selection/tables/batch_by_time.csv):

Cell line is therefore confounded with time except at 48 h, and it is not a covariate in any fit of this book. The two lines are treated as one developmental trajectory (Figure 2.4). Gene ranking and covariance estimation run independently at 48 h, 72 h and 96 h, the slots with a bulk time point (Section 3.4, Section 4.1). At 48 h the two lines are pooled; Section 3.8 measures what pooling changes.

Bulk RNA-seq (GSE229386). The first factor is the Wnt schedule: the standard pulse at 48 h, or an early pulse at 24 h. The second factor is the time point, 48 h, 72 h or 96 h, each with three biological replicates. No single-cell slot carries the early schedule, so the early-Wnt bulk has no single-cell counterpart. The cross-platform filter of Section 3.7 is fitted on the matched pair only: single-cell 48 h against standard-Wnt bulk 48 h.

2.2 Seurat processing

Embeddings, QC and clustering follow the Seurat workflow of the Suppinger resource (Suppinger et al. 2023), drawn in Figure 2.2: Alevin counts, scater QC, SCTransform Pearson residuals per batch for integration, CCA, PCA, UMAP and Louvain clustering. Two products of that workflow are used here: the cell-type labels (celltypeannotation), and the gene list of the authors’ SCT counts layer, which serves as the gene allow-list (Section 3.2). The authors’ residuals themselves are not used: the ranking refits SCTransform v2 per time slot (Section 3.4), and the covariances are estimated from raw counts (Section 4.1.2).

2.3 Biological interpretation from single-cell compositions

Figure 2.3 and Figure 2.4 summarise a putative reading of annotated type proportions along gastruloid time. They are not a reconstruction of embryonic lineages. Two annotated states are treated as technical artefacts of culture stress (Cd63+ ectoderm-like artefact and Zscan4+ artefact, data/dictionaries/suppinger_celltype_crosswalk.csv). The working caudal sequence after the Chir/Wnt pulse is pluripotency \to epiblast \to primitive streak \to caudal mesoderm / NMP \to pre-somitic mesoderm / somites, with a residual endoderm bias typical of gastruloids.

The early-versus-standard Wnt reading of bulk compositions is in Section 7.1, after the mean-based comparators of Section 6.1.

Suppinger, Simon, Marietta Zinner, Nadim Aizarani, et al. 2023. “Multimodal Characterization of Murine Gastruloid Development.” Cell Stem Cell 30 (6): 867–84. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2023.04.018.
